# PAGE 08 — TYPOGRAPHY

## Page title

**TYPOGRAPHY**

## Short body copy

Typography should feel bold, direct and contemporary without becoming loud or gimmicky.

Use two type families only.

### Primary display

**Oswald**

For headlines, campaign statements, experience names and oversized words.

### Secondary body

**Montserrat**

For body copy, navigation, menus, pricing, locations and booking information.

No accent font is required.

## Suggested visual

Show the type hierarchy through a mini specimen:

**GET IN MY BELLY.**

Fire. Food. People.

**BOOK YOUR EXPERIENCE**

## Layout recommendation

Use a large specimen-led page. Display Oswald very large and Montserrat smaller beneath it. Include a simple hierarchy scale.

## Exact text for Canva

**TYPOGRAPHY**

### PRIMARY DISPLAY

**OSWALD**

Bold. Condensed. Confident.

Use for:

**HEADLINES**
**CAMPAIGNS**
**EXPERIENCE NAMES**
**LARGE NUMBERS**
**OVERSIZED WORDS**

### SECONDARY

**MONTSERRAT**

Clear. Modern. Easy to read.

Use for:

Body copy
Navigation
Menus
Pricing
Locations
Booking information

### RULE

**Two type families are enough.**

The photography and headline scale create the personality.

# PAGE 09 — PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTION

## Page title

**PHOTOGRAPHY**

## Short body copy

Photographs should feel like you are standing next to the fire.

Show real food. Real people. Real reactions.

Prioritise **fire, texture, movement, chef personality and gathering**.

Choose authenticity over perfection.

The visual rhythm is:

**MACRO → PERSON → FOOD → FIRE → TABLE → CROWD**

## Suggested visual

A six-image contact sheet showing:

* food macro
* fire
* chef
* hands/carving
* guest reaction
* full table

## Layout recommendation

Use a dynamic image grid with one oversized hero image and smaller supporting images. Avoid rounded-card layouts.

## Exact text for Canva

**PHOTOGRAPHY**

**REAL BEATS PERFECT.**

Photograph the real experience.

Prioritise:

**FIRE**
**FOOD**
**CHEF**
**PEOPLE**
**REACTIONS**

Look for:

Smoke.
Char.
Juices.
Texture.
Carving.
Serving.
Movement.
Laughter.

Keep some imperfection.

**MACRO → PERSON → FOOD → FIRE → TABLE → CROWD**

No stock photography.
No fake event imagery.
No over-retouched food.
