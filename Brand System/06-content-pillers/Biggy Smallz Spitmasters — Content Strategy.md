# BIGGY SMALLZ SPITMASTERS
## Content Strategy

### Content role

The content system should move people through four stages:

**ATTENTION → DESIRE → TRUST → BOOKING**

Not every post needs to sell.

A strong feed should make someone:

- stop scrolling because the food looks incredible
- get to know the chef
- see that real people genuinely enjoy the experience
- understand what Biggy Smallz actually does
- learn something useful
- eventually feel confident enough to enquire

The approved creative direction recommends a broad content mix of approximately **40% food/fire, 25% people/experience, 20% chef/personality, 10% offers/events and 5% brand/editorial**. The six pillars below turn that into a practical publishing system.

---

# PILLAR 01 — GET IN MY BELLY

### Role
**Desire + appetite**

This is the visual engine of the brand.

The job is not to explain the food. The job is to make people **want to eat it**.

### Target audience

Everyone who could potentially buy the experience:

Private hosts, wedding clients, corporate clients, food lovers, festival audiences and private-dining customers.

### Best formats

**Reels, photographs, carousels, Stories**

Use video when there is movement, heat or a satisfying food action. Use photography when one image is strong enough to stop the scroll.

### Ten content ideas

1. Extreme close-up of meat being carved.
2. Slow-motion spit turning over live fire.
3. “First cut” video showing the inside of the meat.
4. Before-and-after: raw/seasoned → cooked.
5. Macro sequence of crust, smoke and juices.
6. “What would you choose?” platter carousel.
7. Food being lifted from the fire and carried to the table.
8. Close-up of sauce, seasoning or finishing.
9. The final overloaded feast table.
10. A ten-second **GET IN MY BELLY** food montage.
