## Two things to avoid in future edits

**No catering jargon.** Not "passionate about food", not "where taste meets
excellence", not "bespoke culinary solutions". The brand's own voice is
short sentences and real nouns: fire, table, courses, people.

**Don't bury the offer.** Whatever gets rewritten later, the first line still
has to say what this is — fire feasts, private dining, seven courses — and
the last line still has to be the booking action. Everything between those
two is negotiable.
