The mark,
and how to use it.

The strategy was already right. What was missing was something you could put on a profile picture, a favicon and an apron. This is it — plus the rules that keep it looking like one brand wherever it lands.

{{SVG_TIP}} The icon

The Spit

Two forms on a skewer — a big one and a small one. It is the spit, and it is Biggy and Smallz. No flames, no badge, nothing off the avoid-list. The pointed tip is load-bearing: without it the mark reads as a dumbbell. Keep the tip, always.

Three cuts, one drawing

Same mark, redrawn for the size it has to survive. Use the smallest cut that still has air around it.

It holds at size

{{SVG_TIP_B}} The wordmark

All three words,
equal weight

Fraunces at weight 900 — a heavy display serif, because a serif says chef, kitchen, table, occasion, where every braai competitor reaches for a condensed sans and ends up looking like a man with a trailer. The three lines lock to one width, so the block keeps its shape at any size. Spitmasters is never a caption.

{{SVG_TIP_C}} Clear space & minimum size

Give it air

One rule, measured off the mark itself so it scales automatically: leave clear space equal to the small form — the little circle on the spit — on all four sides. For the wordmark alone, use the height of the SPITMASTERS caps. Nothing else goes inside that boundary. Not a tagline, not a phone number, not a photo edge.

Minimum clear space — keep everything outside it

Two contrast rules worth knowing

Ash Grey is a label colour, not a reading colour. On charcoal it measures 3.8:1, under the 4.5:1 floor for body text. For secondary text on screen use #8C857E — the same grey lifted to 5.0:1. That is what this page uses.

On an ember field, set type in charcoal, not bone. Charcoal on ember is 4.9:1; bone on ember is 3.1:1 and only safe for large display type. This is why the profile picture is a charcoal spit on orange, not the other way round.

{{SVG_TIP_E}} Type

Two families.
That is enough.

The personality comes from photography, scale and cropping. Typography's job is to be certain, not decorative — so don't add a third face to make something feel creative.

Display  ·  Fraunces 900

GET IN MY BELLY.

Headlines, campaign lines, experience names, the wordmark itself. Uppercase, tight, short. If it needs more than six words, it is body copy.

Text  ·  Archivo 400 / 600

Big fire. Big flavour. Biggy Smallz brings live-fire cooking, generous hospitality and a little theatre to your gathering. Menus, prices, booking information, navigation — everything that has to be read rather than felt.

Accent face
None. Not a script, not a marker, not a chalkboard.

Both are free on Google Fonts. The live site currently runs neither. It ships Cinzel and EB Garamond — the "fine-dining entrance" system picked 2026-08-22 — while 04-colour-type-system still documents Oswald and Montserrat, a pairing that is no longer anywhere. Rolling this type across the site is scheduled separately, so expect three different answers until it happens. The logo files have the type outlined, so they never depend on a font being installed.

One colour to watch before then. The palette on this page is the approved one, and the neutrals match what the site ships almost exactly. The accent does not: Ember here is #E6531A, the site's is #c1432d — deeper and redder. Anything made from this guide will sit beside a site whose orange is visibly different until one of the two moves.
