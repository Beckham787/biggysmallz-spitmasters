## The voice in one line

> **Loud about the eating. Quiet about the care.**

The appetite is shouted: "Get in my belly", exclamation marks, a hashtag. The attention to detail is never mentioned, only shown. [03, 04 §1]

---

## Seven rules

### 1. Talk about the eating, not the cook
Lead with the food and the person eating it. Martin almost never talks about himself as the business.
> ✅ "From spitbraai's to elegant 7 course meals!" [IG-biz]
> ✅ "Get in my belly." [Site]
> ❌ "Chef Biggy personally oversees every detail of your event."

### 2. Show the care. Never claim it.
Hands-on is true, and it's never said. [03] Say what he *does*, and let the reader draw the conclusion.
> ✅ "brought to wherever your table is" [Site]
> ✅ "Message us and Biggy will get back to you himself." (new line, in `bios.md`. It's an action, not a boast, and it's true: he answers every enquiry himself [Vault])
> ❌ "hands-on", "passionate about food", "we love food", "attention to detail" [04 §5]

### 3. The energy is in the punctuation and the appetite, not the adjectives
One exclamation mark, a hashtag, an emoji. No stack of praise words.
> ✅ "…an unforgettable #GetInMyBelly👨🏿‍🍳 experience" [IG-biz, spelling corrected]
> ❌ "premium, world-class, bespoke catering solutions" [04 §5, Studio]

### 4. Name the real thing
Say spitbraai, 7 courses, fire feasts, the venue, the occasion. If a sentence could describe any caterer in Mbombela, it isn't finished.
> ✅ "from fire feasts to a 7-course tasting menu" [Site]
> ❌ "a wide range of catering options for all occasions" (every competitor in 02 §2 says a version of this)

### 5. Discreet about people
Name the venue and the occasion, never a private client. Companies can be named. [Site, `lib/case-studies.ts`]
> ✅ "Wedding · Moyres Venue" [Site, live]
> ❌ A couple's names in a caption, a title, or a link

### 6. Serious when it's hot
Confirmations, timings, dietary questions and anything about the day itself go out plain, exact and joke-free. The fun is for the table, not the logistics. [01 §6, 04 §1]
> ✅ "Confirmed: 120 guests, Saturday 14th, plating from 18:30. Two vegetarian, one no pork." (illustration, not a real booking)
> ❌ "Get in my belly!! See you Saturday 🔥🔥" in a booking confirmation

### 7. Honest about the team
It's "chef @biggy013 **and team**", in his own words. [IG-biz] It's never implied that Biggy cooks every plate. [04 §5] The team can be named. Chef Sikolethu is already named on the site.

## "Get in my belly": where it goes

TK's ruling: **"Biggy makes it fit."** [03] So it isn't banned from weddings or anywhere else. What's needed is a way to say it right in each setting:

| Setting | How it's said |
|---|---|
| Site header, Instagram, hashtag, WhatsApp opener | Full volume. It's the name he chose over his business name [Site] |
| Social captions | At most once per post, usually as the sign-off or the hashtag #GetInMyBelly |
| Proposals, quotes, emails from Biggy | Closed with **"Yours in flavour, Biggy"**. "Get in my belly" can appear once in the body, never as the heading |
| Wedding and event menus | Once, at the foot, as his sign-off |
| Booking confirmations, logistics | Not used (rule 6). "Yours in flavour, Biggy" is fine as the closing |

**Written forms:** "Get in my belly" in running text. **#GetInMyBelly** as a hashtag. **"GET IN MY BELLY"** in capitals only inside his WhatsApp opener, where it's his.

## House style

| | Write | Not | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| The dish | **spitbraai** (one word) | spit braai, spit-braai | Martin's own spelling [IG-biz, Site] |
| Courses | **7-course** / **7 courses** | seven-course | Martin's own [IG-biz, Site] |
| Plural | **spitbraais** | spitbraai's | The apostrophe in the current bio is a typo |
| Martin's title | **Head Honcho** | CEO, Founder, Executive Chef | His own title [IG-own; the site already uses it] |
| Martin, first mention | **Martin "Biggy" Mhlongo**, then **Biggy** | Chef Martin, Mr Mhlongo | [Site] |
| Letter sign-off | **Yours in flavour,** then **Biggy** | "Flavournator" on anything of the brand's | TK, 2026-09-23: Flavournator stays off the brand; "Yours in flavour" instead |

**Words to use:** spitbraai, fire, table, plate, experience, personal, belly, flavour, Head Honcho, "Yours in flavour". [03, IG-biz, IG-own, Site, TK]
**Words to avoid:** Flavournator (his personal account only), hands-on, passion or passionate, "we love food", "not about the money", premium, bespoke, world-class, solutions, "our team of experts", prices and "per person", private client names. [04 §5]

## Found while checking, and fixed at TK's request (2026-09-23)

Two private clients were still named in the site's web addresses. The pages hid the names, but the links didn't: `/work/rob-leah-wedding`, `/work/malinga-40th`, and the image paths `/images/wedding-rob-leah/…` and `/images/malinga/…`. That broke rule 5. Fixed in the site repo, uncommitted, for TK to review:
- The pages are now `/work/wedding-moyres-venue` and `/work/milestone-40th-birthday`. The old addresses 301 to the new ones (`next.config.mjs`), so shared links don't break.
- The image folders and files are renamed to `wedding-moyres/…` and `milestone-40th/…`.
- The client names now survive only in `title:` fields in `lib/case-studies.ts`, which the site never renders (`displayTitle()`), and in the redirect source list.
