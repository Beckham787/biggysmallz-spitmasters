# Biggy Smallz Spitmasters — Stage 2: Research

*Ground · 2026-09-23 · revised the same day with the three competitors TK named, then re-checked: five quotes corrected to the sources' exact wording · approved by TK*

This covers who else sells this locally, what they look like, and which of Biggy's own claims hold up. Every claim links to the page it came from, opened 2026-09-23.

---

## 1. The local spit-braai market is crowded and cheap

- The directory listing for spit-braai caterers in Nelspruit quotes **"R100 – R300/person"** for a braai or spit-braai buffet. Several listed providers are side businesses: one describes itself as "a dedicated construction company", another as "specializing in the installation of garage doors", and a third lists "spit braai catering and funeral catering" among its specialties. — [Procompare, Nelspruit](https://www.procompare.co.za/spit-braai-catering/nelspruit)
- Gauteng specialists reach into the province and sell on volume and packages. Nyama Catering (Pretoria), "Feeding the South African Nation since 2006", sells specials at "R 169" and "R 199 Per Person (Excl. VAT)" and says "Party for 15,000? No problem." — [nyama.co.za](https://nyama.co.za/). Lekker Spit Braai serves "Parts of Mpumalanga" under the tagline "Because it is lekker" — [lekkerspitbraai.co.za](https://lekkerspitbraai.co.za/).

**Reading:** here, "we do spit braai" on its own is a commodity. It's priced low and anyone can list it.

## 3. What this does to the Stage 1 differentiator

The passion for food TK calls "1000% authentic" **can't be won by saying it**, because Umbali already says "we cook because we love food." What none of them shows is *proof of one person*:
- a cook who's been on national TV (Ultimate Braai Master S7, Come Dine With Me, Rate My Plate)
- someone who's been the South African pitmaster at the Mozambique Barbecue Festival
- a part-owner of a restaurant
- someone who fed 5,000 people in Soweto over a Mandela Day week
- and, per TK, someone who knows what's happening in prep even when he isn't on site.

Philip Spitbraai shows that a name alone isn't enough. It's the *evidence behind the name* that nobody locally has.

## 4. Plated fine dining is contested by a platform, and "intimate" by venues

- **Plated/private fine dining:** ChefMaison sells exactly this in Nelspruit, as a marketplace with no named local chef — [ChefMaison](https://chefmaison.com/en-za/services/private-chef/mbombela-nelspruit/). This is where Biggy's plated wedding work competes.
- **"Intimate":** the Mpumalanga wedding directory is mostly bottle stores and décor shops. The one serious entry is a venue, Highgrove House in White River, "famous for small intimate weddings" — [PartiesAndCelebrations](https://www.partiesandcelebrations.co.za/directory/category/catering/wedding-catering-food-drinks/location/mpumalanga/). None of the local spit-braai caterers claims intimacy.

**Reading:** a spit braai served *plated* (TK, Stage 1) sits in a gap. The spit-braai caterers sell buffets and packages. The fine-dining platform sells anonymous chefs and game or omakase menus. Nobody visible sells a plated spit braai by a named pitmaster.

## 5. The culinary-school door is real and local

The Chefs Training & Innovation Academy has a **Mbombela campus** in Riverside Park, with two training kitchens. It says it has put "a great deal of care into forging industry partnerships with luxury lodges and other establishments in the region", and the campus is run by Chef Patrice Seibold. — [ctia.co.za/campuses/mbombela](https://ctia.co.za/campuses/mbombela/)

## 7. What this hands to Stage 3 (observations, not decisions)

1. Spit braai is the anchor, but it can't carry the brand alone because it's priced as a commodity locally.
2. "We love food" is already taken, and a person's name is taken too (Philip). What's open is **the documented cook**: the verified record behind Martin.
3. A plated spit braai sits between the buffet caterers and the anonymous fine-dining platform.
4. The same documented-cook story is the thing a culinary school would partner with.
